# Summary of Changes - August 12, 2026

## 1. Clipping Image Quality & Clarity Enhancements
- **High-Resolution Photo Preservations**: Updated `GenerateScreen.tsx` and `generation.service.ts` to preserve 100% full raw image resolution (up to 3600px width, 1.0 quality) without downscaling uploaded photos.
- **Bi-Cubic Smooth Image Interpolation**: Replaced `-webkit-optimize-contrast` with `image-rendering: auto !important` and `image-rendering: smooth !important` in `master_layout.html` and `render_service.py`.
- **4800px Ultra HD Screenshot Rendering**: Increased Playwright `device_scale_factor` from `3.0` to `4.0` in `render_service.py`, rendering 4800px Ultra HD screenshots so images remain tack-sharp when zoomed in.
- **Removed GPU Layering Artifacts**: Removed `transform: translateZ(0)` and `backface-visibility: hidden` from Playwright injected styles to prevent Chrome from rasterizing images into lower-resolution offscreen GPU textures.

## 2. Application Rebranding ("Spot News 24x7")
- **Capacitor & Android Manifest**: Updated `appName` in `capacitor.config.ts` and `app_name` / `title_activity_main` in `android/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml` to `Spot news 24x7`.
- **Multilingual Support**: Updated translation entries in `src/lib/i18n.ts` across English (`SPOT NEWS 24x7`), Telugu (`స్పాట్ న్యూస్ 24x7`), and Hindi (`స్పొట్ న్యూస్ 24x7`).
- **Header & Ticker Text**: Updated header masthead and red ticker bar text in `App.tsx` to `Welcome to Spot News 24x7`.

## 3. Reporter Profile Picture & Header Layout
- **Pre-Login Reporter Upload**: Added a photo upload card to `LoginScreen.tsx` and `SignupScreen.tsx` so journalists can select their profile picture before signing in.

- **Header Layout**: Positioned the reporter profile photo avatar directly beside `REPORTER: <Name>` on the same horizontal row in `App.tsx`.
- **Dashboard Integration**: Rendered the reporter's uploaded profile picture inside the Welcome Back card avatar in `DashboardScreen.tsx`.

## 4. Storage Quota & App Stability
- **Image Compression**: Added `compressImageToThumbnail` in `src/store/index.ts` to shrink profile picture uploads into tiny ~3–5 KB JPEG thumbnails.
- **Storage Protection**: Created `safeStorage` custom storage wrapper in `src/store/index.ts` to intercept `QuotaExceededError` warnings safely.
- **Store Partialize**: Configured Zustand middleware to exclude large photo strings from `newscraft-auth` local storage payloads.

- **Share Modal Avatar**: Rendered reporter profile picture avatar beside reporter name inside `PreviewScreen.tsx` share modal.
